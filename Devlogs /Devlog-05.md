TLDR: Add new furniture models to map<br><br>
I spent some time filling the empty rooms with some furniture. These rooms included the living room, both bathrooms, and dining room. The furniture makes the house feel more real and increases the realistic aspect of my game.
I added the ability for players to sit on couches chairs and toliets, these act as obstacles in the enviroment that can make players stuck if they are not careful. 
I also fixed a bug where the sparkle effect around the key found outside was not present. This bug occured because I did not put the effect in the key handle, so instead of beign aorund the model it was in the center of the map.
To fix it I simply moved the effect into the handle and the effect appeared around the key.
